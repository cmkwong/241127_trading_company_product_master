# Product Master

Product Master is a core business system used to maintain and manage comprehensive product information, such as product specifications, supplier details, pricing, costs, and related commercial data. The system serves as a single source of truth for product records, ensuring that users can access accurate and consistent information across different business processes.

In addition to product data management, Product Master provides functional support for generating sales quotations and purchase requests. This allows users to prepare customer quotations more efficiently and initiate procurement activities based on reliable product and supplier information. Overall, the system helps streamline sales and purchasing workflows, improve data consistency, and support better decision-making within the organization.
